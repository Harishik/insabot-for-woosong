# Demo Script

1. Open the home screen and show the WSU AI Assistant entry card.
2. Ask: “How do I record my attendance?”
3. Show verified answer, steps, citations, voice/TTS controls, save, copy, and feedback.
4. Ask an unverifiable question and show the safe fallback.
5. Open human support and route to the correct office.
6. Switch language and explain multilingual support.
7. Open the personalized student dashboard.
8. Open notices and explain notice summaries.
9. Open dark chat mode.
10. Open admin dashboard and show analytics, failed questions, source approvals, and announcement sync.
